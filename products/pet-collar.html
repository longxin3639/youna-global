How to Build Your Custom Pet Collar Line

1. Choose Your Colors & Sizes

Select from our 6 standard colors (black, red, blue, green, orange, pink) or provide PMS color codes for custom matching. Pick 2–4 size options to cover small dogs to large breeds.

2. Add Your Brand Logo

We offer 4 branding methods: woven logo ribbon (most premium), screen printing (most affordable), laser engraving on buckle, and custom embroidery. Send us your AI or PDF file — our design team creates a free mockup within 24 hours.

3. Select Packaging

Choose from polybag + header card (budget-friendly), backer card (retail display), or custom printed box (premium). We design the packaging layout at no extra charge for orders of 1,000+ pcs.

4. Approve Sample & Ship

We produce a physical sample for your approval in 5–7 days. Once approved, bulk production starts immediately. You receive real-time photos and video updates throughout production.

Why Choose Our Pet Collars

Heavy-Duty Zinc Alloy Buckle

Die-cast zinc alloy buckles withstand 200+ N pull tension. Pass 5,000+ open/close cycle tests. Safety release mechanism prevents choking if the collar gets snagged on branches or fences.

Premium Nylon Webbing

High-density nylon with reinforced stitching. Washable, fade-resistant, and comfortable for daily wear — even for dogs that pull. UV coating prevents color fading after months of sun exposure.

4 Sizes, Fully Adjustable

S (25–35 cm), M (35–45 cm), L (45–55 cm), XL (55–65 cm) with slide adjustment. One collar fits growing puppies or weight-fluctuating adult dogs. Clear size chart included in every package.

Custom Colors & Logo

Custom PMS color matching, woven logo ribbon, embroidery, or screen printing. Build your brand with low MOQ flexible customization. Free design mockup within 24 hours.

D-Ring & Name Tag Loop

Welded D-ring for leash attachment withstands 150+ kg pull force. Optional name tag loop holds standard ID tags or GPS trackers. Both are riveted through the nylon webbing, not glued.

3 Hardware Finishes

Nickel (classic silver), black matte (modern stealth look), or rose gold (premium aesthetic). All finishes are rust-resistant and pass 48-hour salt spray testing. Match your brand identity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Standard MOQ is 300 pcs per color and size combination. For mixed containers with leashes or harnesses, we can negotiate lower MOQs for each SKU. Orders of 5,000+ pcs qualify for an additional 5% volume discount.

Can I get samples before bulk ordering?

Yes — we provide 1–2 free samples (you pay shipping, ~$15–25 via DHL). Sample lead time is 5–7 days. If you proceed with a bulk order of 300+ pcs, we refund the sample shipping cost as a credit on your invoice.

Is the buckle truly durable?

Our zinc alloy buckles are the same specification used by leading US pet brands. They pass 5,000+ open/close cycle tests and 200N tension tests. We also offer a quick-release plastic buckle option for lightweight dogs under 10 kg.

Do you provide retail packaging?

Yes — options include polybag with header card, backer card, or custom color box with your branding. Packaging MOQ starts at 1,000 pcs. We can also ship bulk (no packaging) for Amazon FBA sellers who do their own packaging.

What is the production lead time?

10–18 days after sample approval and deposit. Shipping adds 5–7 days (air) or 25–35 days (sea) to your door. We provide real-time production photos and videos for every order so you can track progress.

Can I order matching leashes with the same design?

Absolutely — we produce matching leashes using the same nylon webbing and hardware. Leash lengths: 120 cm (standard) or 150 cm (long). MOQ 300 pcs for leashes. Many buyers order collars + leashes as a combo set.

What is the warranty and after-sales support?

12-month warranty on hardware (buckle, D-ring). If buckles break under normal use, we replace them free of charge in your next shipment. Stitching issues are covered for 6 months. We provide detailed photo guides for end-customer size selection.

How is shipping handled for bulk orders?

Pet collars are extremely lightweight (~50 g/pc) and compact. 1,000 pcs = ~1 carton (50×40×40 cm, ~25 kg). Sea freight cost is negligible — under $0.20/unit even for small orders. We handle export documentation and can arrange DDP delivery to your door.
