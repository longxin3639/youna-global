How It Works - Smart Feeding in 3 Steps

1. Download the App

Download the free companion app from App Store or Google Play. Connect the feeder to your 2.4 GHz WiFi in under 60 seconds. No smart hub or subscription required.

2. Set Your Schedule

Program up to 6 meals per day with adjustable portion sizes (5-50 g per meal). Set different schedules for weekdays and weekends. The feeder stores schedules locally, so it works even if WiFi drops.

3. Your Pet Gets Fed

At mealtime, the dispenser rotates and drops the exact portion into the stainless steel bowl. Your recorded voice message plays automatically - your pet hears you call them for dinner, even when you are away.

Bonus: Real-Time Alerts

Get push notifications when food is low, when a meal is dispensed, or if the feeder jams. Remote manual feed button lets you give an extra snack anytime from your phone.

Professional Pet Feeder Sourcing: Beyond the App

As the smart pet product market explodes, importing automatic feeders from China requires a keen eye for technical stability. At Youna Global, we don't just look at the exterior; we analyze the "guts" of the machine to ensure your customers are satisfied.

1. App Stability & Server Reliability

A smart feeder is only as good as its app. Many cheap manufacturers use unstable third-party cloud services that crash, leaving pets hungry. We recommend factories that use Tuya or proprietary stable servers with global AWS nodes. We test the app's responsiveness and notification speed during our factory audits.

2. Anti-Jam Mechanism & Motor Quality

The #1 complaint for auto feeders is food jamming. We source models with high-torque motors and reverse-rotation logic. If a kibble gets stuck, the machine automatically reverses and retries, ensuring the meal is always dispensed. We verify this with various kibble sizes (from 2mm to 15mm).

3. Material Safety & Hygiene (Stainless Steel vs. Plastic)

While plastic bowls are cheaper, they often cause "cat acne" due to bacterial buildup. We strongly advise our clients to source models with removable 304 stainless steel bowls. They are dishwasher-safe, more hygienic, and significantly increase the product's perceived value on Amazon and retail shelves.

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of food can I use in this feeder?

Dry food only - kibble with diameter 2-15 mm. Semi-wet or wet food will jam the dispenser mechanism. We recommend kibble smaller than 12 mm for the smoothest, most reliable operation. The stainless steel bowl is easy to clean if food residue builds up.

What happens if the WiFi disconnects?

The feeder stores your feeding schedule locally on the device. If WiFi drops, it continues dispensing meals on schedule automatically. It reconnects to WiFi when the signal returns. You can also press the manual feed button on the device at any time.

How long does the battery backup last?

3x D-batteries (not included) provide 7-10 days of backup power. We strongly recommend keeping the DC adapter plugged in at all times and using batteries only as emergency backup for power outages. The LED display shows battery status.
