Product Details

This LED ceiling fan with light combines efficient air circulation with modern LED lighting in one unit. Sourced directly from Foshan lighting factories, it features a DC brushless motor for quiet operation and energy savings. The 3-color adjustable LED lets users switch between warm, natural, and cool white light. Ideal for wholesale buyers, home improvement retailers, and brand owners looking for OEM customization.

Key Features

  • DC brushless motor - 30% more energy efficient than AC motors, operates below 35dB
  • 3-color adjustable LED (3000K/4000K/6000K) - switch between warm, natural, and cool white
  • Reverse function - push air down for summer cooling, pull air up for winter circulation
  • Remote control included - 6 speed levels, light on/off, color temperature, timer
  • ABS blades - moisture-resistant, durable, won't warp in humid environments

Frequently Asked Questions

What sizes are available for the LED ceiling fan? +

We offer 42-inch, 48-inch, 52-inch, and 60-inch blade diameters. The 52-inch is the most popular for standard rooms (15-25 sqm). For larger spaces like living rooms or halls, the 60-inch is recommended.

Is the LED light dimmable or color-adjustable? +

Yes. Most models support 3-color temperature adjustment: warm white (3000K), natural white (4000K), and cool white (6000K). Some premium models also support stepless dimming via remote control.

Can I put my own brand logo on the fan and packaging? +

Absolutely. Custom logo printing on the fan body and retail packaging starts at MOQ 200 pcs. We also offer custom blade colors, custom remote control design, and branded user manuals.

What certifications do these ceiling fans have? +

Our factory partners hold CE, RoHS, and CCC certifications. For specific markets like the US (UL/ETL) or Middle East (SASO), we can arrange additional certification testing. Please confirm your target market requirements.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +

Standard MOQ is 50 pcs per model. Custom branding starts at 200 pcs. Mixed-size orders within the same model are possible. For orders above 500 pcs, we offer volume discounts.

Do you ship ceiling fans worldwide? +

Yes. We ship to the USA, Europe, UK, Australia, Middle East, Southeast Asia, and 20+ countries. Shipping options include express air (5-10 days), standard air (10-15 days), and sea freight (25-40 days). DDP shipping with duties prepaid is available.
