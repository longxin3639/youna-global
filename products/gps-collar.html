How accurate is the GPS tracking?

In open sky (parks, fields): 3-5 m accuracy. In urban areas with tall buildings: 10-20 m. Indoors with Wi-Fi: 15-30 m. The 4-mode positioning (GPS+BD+Wi-Fi+LBS) automatically selects the best method for each environment, ensuring the best possible accuracy at all times.

Can the collar be used for cats?

Yes - the collar fits necks from 25 cm (small dog/cat) to 65 cm (large dog). For cats under 5 kg, we recommend our lightweight GPS tracker (40 g) - ask us for details. The current model weighs ~65 g which is comfortable for medium-to-large cats.

What is the warranty?

12-month warranty on the tracker module. Water damage is covered if the sealed charging port was not improperly opened. Defective units are replaced in your next order (no need to return). We also provide spare batteries and replacement collars at cost price.
