Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real 15-20 bar or fake pressure? +

Real. The piston pump reaches 15-20 bar, the standard espresso range. Unlike cheap $5-10 portable models that claim "high pressure" but actually deliver 5-8 bar, ours pulls a true shot with crema.

What is the battery life? +

A 1500-2000 mAh built-in battery charges via USB-C in about 2 hours and delivers 30-40 brews per charge (single shot, 50-80 ml each). One charge covers a full work week of commuting.

What ground coffee or capsules does it use? +

Two modes: ground coffee (NSR / CSR reusable basket) or NSR-compatible capsules. Ground coffee is preferred for taste; capsules are preferred for convenience on the go.

Is OEM / private label supported? +

Yes. Custom logo (laser engraving or silk-screen), color-matched body shell (MOQ 500 units), retail box, and user manual. Lead time is 30-40 days for color customization.

What about CE / FCC certification for export? +

Pre-certified: CE, FCC, and RoHS. The cup interior is FDA food-grade 304 stainless. A documentation bundle is supplied with the shipment - no additional certification cost.

Can I bundle with my e-commerce store brand? +

Yes. Many Amazon FBA and Shopify sellers bundle our units as "espresso starter kits" with name-brand ground coffee. We supply the retail box, manual, and FNSKU labels on request.
