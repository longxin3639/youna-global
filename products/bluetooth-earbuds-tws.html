Sourcing TWS Earbuds from China: The Sourcing Agent's Guide

Shenzhen is the world's capital for Bluetooth earbuds, but with thousands of factories, quality variance is extreme. As your sourcing partner on the ground, we navigate the "spec trap" to find reliable products that protect your brand's reputation.

1. The Truth About Specs: Real vs. Fake

It's common for suppliers to claim "10 hours playtime" or "ANC -35dB" when the reality is half that. We perform Battery Stress Tests and Decibel Testing in our office to verify these claims before you commit to a 1,000-unit order.

2. Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) Tiers

Not all ANC is equal. We categorize earbuds into three levels for our clients:

  • Entry Level: Software-based noise reduction (CVC 8.0). Best for budget promos.
  • Mid-Range: Feedback ANC (approx. -20dB). Good for general office use.
  • Premium: Hybrid ANC (Feedforward + Feedback, -35dB to -40dB). Compares to top-tier brands.

3. Custom Branding & White Labeling

We specialize in helping Amazon FBA and Shopify sellers create their own private label brands. This includes:

  • Laser Engraving: High-precision logo placement on the charging case.
  • Custom Bluetooth Pairing Name: When your customer connects, they see "YOUR BRAND" on their phone screen.
  • Tailored Voice Prompts: Custom start-up sounds or voice alerts in any language.
