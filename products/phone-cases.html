Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom phone cases? +

Standard MOQ is 100 pcs per case style/color for generic (unbranded) orders. Custom logo printing starts at 200 pcs per design. Mixed-style orders are welcome - you can combine any of the 7 case types in one shipment, and orders above 1,000 units qualify for tiered volume discounts.

Can you print my logo on the phone cases? +

Yes. We offer custom logo phone cases with pad printing, UV printing or laser engraving on the back or side. Packaging can also be customized (PE bag, retail box, or branded clamshell). This is our core OEM/private-label service for resellers and Amazon sellers.

Which phone models are your cases compatible with? +

We produce cases compatible with iPhone series, Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Huawei, Xiaomi, OPPO, OnePlus, vivo, Realme and Motorola, across current and recent generations. Send us your target model list and we will confirm mold availability.

What materials are your phone cases made of? +

Our phone case line covers clear TPU (standard and reinforced), colorful TPU, dual-layer PC+TPU armor, TPU+PC kickstand, glossy PC hard shell, and heavy-duty shockproof cases.

Do you offer heavy-duty armor and shockproof phone cases? +

Yes. Our Armor Case and Armor Shockproof Case use dual-layer PC+TPU with reinforced frames and air-cushion corners for military-grade drop protection. Both support OEM logo printing and custom retail packaging.
