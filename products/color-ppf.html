TPU Color PPF — Paint Protection Meets Color Transformation

Color PPF (Colored Paint Protection Film) is the fastest-growing category in the automotive film market — combining the protective performance of traditional clear PPF with a full-body color change effect. Our TPU-based Color PPF features self-healing technology, hydrophobic top coat, and anti-yellowing properties, available in 500+ colors across gloss, metallic/chameleon, and matte finishes. Standard roll is 1.52m × 15m, priced at $300–350 per roll FOB Guangdong.

Color PPF is fundamentally different from vinyl wrap. While vinyl (PVC-based) lasts 2–3 years and offers no real protection, TPU Color PPF provides 5–7 years of durability with genuine self-healing impact protection. For detailers and distributors, Color PPF commands a significantly higher retail price and margin compared to both vinyl wrap and standard clear PPF. Frequently Asked Questions

What is the price per roll for wholesale Color PPF? +

Standard TPU Color PPF (gloss, metallic, or matte) is $300–$350 per roll (1.52m × 15m) FOB Guangdong. Chameleon color-shift films are at the upper end of this range. Bulk pricing available for orders of 20+ rolls. Contact us for a precise quotation based on your selected colors and quantity.

How many rolls does a full car wrap require? +

A standard sedan typically requires 15–18 meters (1 roll) for a full-body Color PPF application. Larger SUVs and trucks may need 20–22 meters. Our standard roll (1.52m × 15m) is sufficient for most passenger vehicles.

Can I mix different colors in one order? +

Yes — mixed color orders are welcome. You can order multiple colors in a single shipment without any minimum per color. This is ideal for detailers building a color wall display or testing which colors sell best in your market.

Is installation the same as vinyl wrap? +

Color PPF installation follows the same basic principles as vinyl wrap but requires more skill — the TPU material is thicker and has different stretch characteristics. Experienced PPF/vinyl installers can adapt quickly. We provide installation guidance and can recommend training resources for your team.

Can I get color samples before ordering? +

Yes. We offer sample swatches (A4 size) for any color in our catalogue. Sample cost covers material and express shipping. If you proceed with a bulk order, we can credit the sample cost against your first order. Digital color charts are available immediately upon request.

Does Color PPF really self-heal like clear PPF? +

Yes — our Color PPF uses the same TPU substrate technology as premium clear PPF. Light surface scratches and swirl marks disappear when heat is applied (sunlight, warm water, or heat gun). The embedded color layer does not affect the self-healing performance of the TPU top layer.
