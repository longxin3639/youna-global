Frequently Asked Questions

Does this work exactly like an Apple AirTag? +

Yes - it connects to the Apple Find My network just like an AirTag. You can see it on the map, play a sound to locate it, and receive left-behind notifications. Android users can use the Smart Find app or similar third-party apps.

What is the Bluetooth tracking range? +

The direct Bluetooth range is up to 100 meters in open space. Beyond that, it relies on the Find My network - any nearby Apple device will anonymously relay its location back to you.

How long does the battery last? +

The replaceable CR2032 coin battery lasts approximately 10-12 months with normal use. When it runs low, you will get a notification in the app. Replacing the battery takes seconds.

Can I put my own logo on the tracker? +

Absolutely. We offer custom logo printing starting at MOQ 500 pcs. Options include silk-screen printing, UV printing, and laser engraving. Custom packaging is also available.
