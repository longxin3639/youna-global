Frequently Asked Questions

What fabrics are available for the sleepwear sets? +

We offer three main fabric options: 100% cotton (soft, breathable, year-round), silk-like polyester (smooth, luxurious feel, wrinkle-resistant), and modal (ultra-soft, cooling, premium). Each fabric suits different price points and target markets.

Can I order mixed sizes and colors in one shipment? +

Yes. We encourage mixed-size and mixed-color orders so you can build a complete inventory. Minimum 50 sets per size/color per pattern. This lets you offer your customers a full range without overcommitting to any single SKU.

Do you offer 3-piece sets with robes? +

Yes. We can add a matching robe or kimono to create a 3-piece gift set. This is popular for holiday gift sets and premium retail. The robe adds approximately $1.50-$3.00 to the set price depending on fabric and length.

What is the production lead time? +

For in-stock items, orders ship within 5-7 business days. Custom OEM orders with your brand and design take 15-20 business days depending on order volume. Rush orders (7-10 days) are available for an additional fee.

Do you ship worldwide? +

Yes. We ship to the USA, Europe, UK, Australia, Canada, Middle East, Southeast Asia, and 20+ other countries. Options include express air (5-10 days), standard air (10-15 days), and sea freight (25-40 days). We also offer DDP shipping with duties prepaid.
