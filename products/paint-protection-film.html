Finish Options

Gloss vs. Matte — Which Finish?

Choose the finish based on your end customer's preference and target vehicle type.

Gloss Finish

The classic clear bra look — optically invisible protection that preserves the factory paint finish and deepens gloss depth.

  • ✓ Near-invisible on most paint colours
  • ✓ Deepens paint gloss (wet-look effect)
  • ✓ Best seller worldwide
  • ✓ Suitable for all vehicle types

Matte / Satin Finish

Transforms gloss-paint vehicles to a premium satin look — no permanent paint modification required. Increasingly popular on luxury and performance cars.

  • ✓ Transforms gloss to satin without repainting
  • ✓ Trending in EU, Middle East, Asia
  • ✓ Higher retail margin than gloss
  • ✓ Available in wider rolls for full-body coverage

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for wholesale PPF? +

Minimum order is 1 roll (1.52m × 15m) for standard stock. For OEM packaging with your brand, MOQ is typically 50–100 rolls depending on the grade. Mix different grades to reach the OEM minimum — they count together.

What is the difference between TPU and PC PPF? +

TPU (thermoplastic polyurethane) is the traditional PPF material — flexible, self-healing, and cost-effective. PC (polycarbonate) uses a harder substrate that offers stronger impact resistance, superior chemical resistance, and warranties up to 15 years. PC PPF is harder to install but commands a higher retail price.

Which substrate should I choose — BASF, Covestro, Lubrizol, or Wanhua? +

It depends on your target market. Wanhua: best value for price-sensitive markets. Covestro: mid-to-premium, well known globally. BASF: premium branding value, popular with European distributors. Lubrizol: used by XPEL and 3M — highest brand recognition among professional installers worldwide. Contact us for a sample kit to compare.

Can I get samples before placing a bulk order? +

Yes. We can provide sample rolls (typically 1–2 meters) from each grade for testing. Sample cost covers material and shipping — if you proceed with a bulk order, we can credit the sample cost against your first order.

How long does shipping take? +

Express air freight: 5–8 business days to USA/Europe. Standard air freight: 10–15 days. Sea freight (for large orders, 200+ rolls): 20–35 days. We also offer DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) shipping — you receive the goods with all import duties cleared. We are based in Guangdong, China, close to Shenzhen and Guangzhou ports.
