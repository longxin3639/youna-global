How It Works — Night Safety in 3 Steps

1. Charge the Collar

Plug in the included USB-C cable. Full charge in 1.5 hours. The LED indicator turns green when fully charged. A single charge lasts 6–12 hours depending on the light mode you select.

2. Press to Activate

One button press cycles through 3 modes: steady glow (everyday walks), slow flash (jogging), fast flash (maximum visibility). The collar remembers your last mode setting between uses.

3. Walk Safely at Night

The LED tube is visible from 500+ meters in the dark. Cars, cyclists, and other walkers see your dog from far away. Rain-proof IPX4 rating means no worries about wet evening walks.

Bonus: Dual-Purpose Design

During the day, the LED tube reflects ambient light like a regular reflective collar. At night, the LED makes your dog glow. No need for a separate day collar and night collar.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the battery last per charge?

6–12 hours depending on mode. Steady glow: ~10–12 h. Slow flash: ~8–10 h. Fast flash: ~6–8 h. The collar ships with a USB-C charging cable. A full recharge takes only 1.5 hours.

Can I customize the webbing color and logo?

Yes — custom PMS webbing colors at 500+ pcs. Logo can be printed on the buckle (laser engraving) or on the webbing (screen print). We provide a free digital mockup within 24 hours. Sample custom units take 7–10 days to produce.

Is it safe for small dogs?

Yes — the LED module is low-voltage (3.7V) and fully sealed. The collar webbing is soft nylon, comfortable for small breeds (XS fits necks from 25 cm). We recommend removing the collar when the dog is unattended for long periods.

What is the warranty policy?

We provide 12-month warranty on the LED module and battery. Defective units are replaced in your next order (we do not require returns — just send photos/videos). This keeps your after-sales cost low and processing fast.

Can I mix sizes and colors in one order?

Yes — MOQ is 300 pcs total. You can split by size and color (e.g., 100 red + 100 blue + 100 green). For custom PMS colors, each color requires 500+ pcs. This flexibility helps you test which colors sell best before committing to larger quantities.

What is the production lead time?

12–18 days after sample approval and deposit. Shipping adds 5–7 days (air) or 25–35 days (sea). We send production photos and a video of your finished order before shipping so you can verify quality.

Can I get samples before bulk ordering?

Yes — we provide 1–2 free samples (you pay DHL shipping, ~$20–30). Sample lead time is 5–7 days. If you proceed with a bulk order of 300+ pcs, we credit the sample shipping cost against your invoice.

How is shipping handled for bulk orders?

LED collars are lightweight (~80 g/pc) and compact. 500 pcs = ~1 carton (50×40×35 cm, ~20 kg). Sea freight is extremely cost-effective — under $0.30/unit even for small orders. We handle export documentation and can arrange DDP delivery to your door.
