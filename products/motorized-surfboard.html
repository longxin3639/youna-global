Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +

MOQ is 1 unit. You can order a single sample or demo unit. Volume discounts apply at 10 units ($3,600/unit) and 100 units ($3,200/unit), making this suitable for both testing and bulk distribution.

How much does a wholesale motorized surfboard cost? +

Reference factory prices range from $3,200/unit at 100-unit volume up to $4,999/unit for a single unit, FOB Shenzhen/Dongguan. Final pricing depends on hull material, battery capacity, motor power and custom branding.

What is included with the motorized surfboard? +

Each unit includes the motorized surfboard hull, high-capacity lithium battery, wireless handheld controller, battery charger, safety leash, and a wooden export crate for shipping. Branded packaging, user manual and custom color/decal can be added for OEM orders.

What are the weight and shipping dimensions? +

Board weight: 30 kg for fiberglass hull, 24 kg for carbon fiber hull. Battery weight: 28-30 kg. Wooden crate: 20 kg. Charger and accessories: 3 kg. Total gross shipping weight is roughly 75-83 kg depending on configuration. Export carton dimensions: 36 cm (H) x 48 cm (W) x 183 cm (L); volumetric weight is approximately 53 kg.

Can you do private label or custom branding? +

Yes. We offer OEM and private-label electric surfboards with custom deck graphics, hull colors, printed logos, branded packaging, user manuals and controller decals. This lets you launch your own watersports or lifestyle brand without the factory overhead.

Who buys motorized surfboards from your factory? +

Our buyers include watersports rental operators, beach resorts, marinas, yacht clubs, Amazon/Shopify DTC brands, TikTok/e-commerce sellers, and outdoor distributors targeting high-ticket adventure and watersports consumers.

Do you provide samples or a video call? +

Yes. We can provide factory photos, product videos and live video calls. Physical sample units are available at 1-unit price plus shipping. Sample lead time is typically 7-12 days after payment.
