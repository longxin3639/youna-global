Frequently Asked Questions

Is this natural Jadeite or treated?
We source both Grade A (untreated, only cut and polished) and Grade B/C (polymer-impregnated or dyed) depending on your request and budget. Every piece comes with a clear grade declaration, and we can arrange third-party lab certification at your expense if needed.
Where does the Jadeite come from?
Our material originates from Myanmar (Burma) mines and enters China through official trade channels into Guangzhou, which is the world's largest Jadeite processing and trading hub. We do not deal with unregulated or conflict-sourced material.
Can I see the actual piece before buying?
Yes - absolutely. For any piece over $5,000, we provide live video calls showing the Jadeite under different lighting conditions (natural light, flashlight, transmitted light). You can request additional angles, close-ups of inclusions, and comparison shots alongside reference pieces.
What is the minimum order?
MOQ is 1 piece. Whether you want a single pendant as a gift or a complete bridal set, we handle individual orders. For retailers ordering multiple pieces, volume discounts apply.
Do you offer OEM / white label?
Yes - we can package each piece with your brand's certificate, gift box, and tag. This is popular with jewelry retailers, auction houses, and e-commerce sellers who sell under their own label. Contact us for OEM pricing and lead times.
How long does the process take?
In-stock pieces: 3-7 days from selection to shipment. Custom carvings or sets requiring new production: 2-6 weeks depending on complexity. Shipping time varies by destination (air courier 3-7 days, sea freight 20-40 days).
What if the piece arrives different from the photos?
We photograph every piece extensively before packing and include those photos in the shipment record. In the rare event of a discrepancy, we work with you on return, exchange, or refund according to our terms. All shipments above $10,000 include insurance covering transit damage.
