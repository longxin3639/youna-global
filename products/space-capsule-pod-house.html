What Is a Space Capsule Pod House?

The Space Capsule Pod House is a fully prefabricated modular living unit manufactured in China and shipped worldwide. Designed as a turnkey accommodation solution, these futuristic pods combine cutting-edge architecture with practical engineering. Each unit arrives pre-wired, pre-plumbed, and 90% assembled, requiring only minimal on-site foundation work and utility connections before it is move-in ready.

Unlike traditional container houses or foldable cabins, the Space Capsule Pod features a curved aerodynamic shell with panoramic floor-to-ceiling glass, creating a premium guest experience that commands higher nightly rates for hospitality operators. The pod’s Instagram-worthy aesthetic makes it a natural magnet for social media marketing, driving organic bookings for glamping sites and boutique resorts.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take from order to delivery?
Production takes 25-45 days after design approval, depending on customization level. Sea freight adds 20-45 days depending on destination. Total timeline is typically 45-90 days from deposit payment to port delivery.
Do I need a building permit for a capsule pod?
Permit requirements vary by country, state, and municipality. In many jurisdictions, small prefabricated units under a certain size may qualify as temporary structures or ADUs with simplified permitting. We recommend checking your local building codes. We can provide structural engineering certificates to support permit applications.
What foundation is required?
The pods can be placed on a flat concrete pad, pier foundations, or even steel stilts for sloped terrain. The minimum requirement is a level surface that can support 3-5 tons. For temporary installations, ground screws or helical piles work well without permanent concrete work.
Can the pods be stacked for multi-story buildings?
Yes, select models are designed with stackable steel frames that allow 2-3 story configurations. Stackable designs require additional structural reinforcement and specialized connection brackets. Contact us with your project details for engineering recommendations.
How are the pods shipped?
Each pod is loaded into a standard shipping container (20ft or 40ft HQ). A 40ft HQ container typically fits 1-2 units depending on size. We handle container loading supervision, lashing, and customs documentation. Shipping is available to all major ports worldwide.
Can I customize the pod with my own brand?
Absolutely. We support full OEM/white-label customization including custom exterior colors with your brand RAL code, branded interior signage, logo engraving on glass and metal surfaces, and custom furniture packages with your branding. Resort chains often order pods with fully branded interiors.
What is the warranty?
Standard factory warranty covers structural integrity for 5 years and waterproofing for 3 years. Electrical and plumbing components carry the manufacturer’s warranty (typically 1-2 years). Extended warranty options are available for resort and commercial projects.
Can the pods operate off-grid?
Yes, with the optional off-grid package: roof-mounted solar panels (1-3kW), lithium battery storage, water tank with pump, and composting or incinerating toilet. This makes the pods suitable for remote locations without utility connections.
