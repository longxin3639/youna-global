Frequently Asked Questions

What file format do you need for my design? +

We accept AI, EPS, SVG, PDF, and high-resolution PNG (300+ DPI). If you only have a rough sketch, our design team can help create print-ready artwork for a small fee. For DTG printing, we can also work with JPEG files.

Can I order multiple designs in one order? +

Yes. We support mixed-design orders. Minimum 50 pcs per design for DTG, 200 pcs per design for screen printing. This lets you test multiple designs without committing to large quantities of any single one.

Will the print fade after washing? +

Our screen prints and DTG prints are designed to last 50+ washes without significant fading. We use water-based and plastisol inks that bond deeply with the fabric. For longest life, we recommend washing inside-out in cold water.

What is the production lead time? +

Standard production takes 10-15 business days after artwork approval. Rush orders (5-7 days) are available for an additional fee. Shipping time is separate and depends on your chosen method (express, air, or sea).

Do you ship worldwide? +

Yes. We ship to the USA, Europe, UK, Australia, Canada, Middle East, Southeast Asia, and 20+ other countries. Options include express air (5-10 days), standard air (10-15 days), and sea freight (25-40 days). We also offer DDP shipping with duties prepaid.
