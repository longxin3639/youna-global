Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real bean-to-cup or capsule-only? +

Real bean-to-cup. A 1.2 kg bean hopper, conical burr grinder, and 15 bar espresso pump brew fresh drinks from whole beans. No capsules required - a touchscreen boiler also serves hot water for tea and american.

What payment systems are supported? +

MDB protocol standard with a coin acceptor and bill validator installed by default. Cashless readers - Nayax, Ingenico, Verifone, PAX - are available as an add-on of $80-200 per unit. QR payments (WeChat / Alipay) can be wired through the cashless reader.

Can I track inventory remotely? +

Yes. An optional 4G / WiFi telemetry module ($120/unit) reports bean hopper level, water tank level, paper cup count, drainage status, sales analytics, and error logs. It comes with a web dashboard for route management.

What about service and maintenance? +

A daily self-cleaning cycle runs automatically. Descaling is needed every 3-6 months, guided on the touchscreen. Spare parts (grinder burr, pump, group head gasket) are stocked for 7 years, so long-term service is covered.

Can I customize the front panel with my brand? +

Yes. MOQ is 20 units for OEM: front panel wrap (3M vinyl or factory-painted), UI re-skin (your logo, menu items, colors), and payment-module pre-installation. Lead time is 45-60 days.

What is the typical payback period for a vending operator? +

Industry average is 12-18 months. Cup cost runs about $0.15-0.25 while retail price is $2.50-4.00 per cup. At an average of 80 cups per day, that is roughly $200-300 daily gross, or about $5K-7K monthly per machine.
