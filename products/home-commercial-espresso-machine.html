Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a real 15 bar pump or a marketing claim? +

Real. It uses an Italian ULKA pump rated at 15 bar working pressure (9 bar brew pressure, the standard espresso spec). It is not the "20 bar marketing" claim you see on cheap units that only deliver 9 bar or less in practice.

Can I order a custom UI menu for my brand? +

Yes. MOQ is 500 units for a custom UI re-skin: your logo, your menu items, your color theme, and multi-language support (EN / DE / FR / ES / IT / JP / CN). Lead time is +25 days.

What is the difference between this and a Breville or De'Longhi? +

Same Italian ULKA pump, same STMicro controller, same pressure-gauge spec, built in the same Guangdong kitchen-appliance cluster. The difference is brand premium - you pay for the name, not the components.

Can it run on 110V (US) and 220V (EU)? +

Both. The OEM SKU has a dual-voltage PSU (110-120V / 220-240V selectable at the factory). Default is 220V; a US plug adds inquire for pricing.

What about warranty and spare parts? +

12-month limited warranty. Spare parts (pump, grinder burr, gaskets, steam wand) are available for 5 years post-shipment. We ship via DHL / UPS from the Shenzhen service hub.

How long is the lead time? +

Stock models ship in 15-25 days. Custom color or UI adds 30-45 days. Sea freight to the US or EU adds 25-35 days. Total door-to-door for non-stock is about 50-80 days.
