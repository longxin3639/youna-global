Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for inflatable SUP boards? +

MOQ is 50 units for standard all-round and touring inflatable SUP boards. Multi-person XL models start at 20 units. Mixed container orders (combining SUP colors, sizes and accessories) are accepted for total MOQ 100 units, giving you flexibility to test different styles.

What is included in the SUP board complete kit? +

Each inflatable SUP board comes as a complete kit: drop-stitch PVC board, 3-piece adjustable aluminum or carbon paddle, dual-action hand pump, coiled ankle leash, removable center fin + side fins, repair kit, and a durable carry backpack. OEM packaging and branded accessories can be added.

Can you do private label and custom design for my paddleboard brand? +

Yes. We offer OEM/private-label inflatable SUP boards with custom deck pads, printed logos, branded carry bags, color-matched paddles, printed user manuals and branded packaging. You can also develop custom sizes (e.g., 10'6, 11', 12', 15' multi-person) and shapes.

How much does a wholesale inflatable SUP board from China cost? +

Reference factory prices range from $80/unit for all-round beginner models up to $150/unit for premium touring, yoga and multi-person boards, FOB Shenzhen/Dongguan. Final pricing depends on board size, layer thickness, paddle material, accessories level and order quantity. Contact us for a precise quote.

Are your SUP boards certified and what is the quality standard? +

Our SUP boards are built with high-density drop-stitch PVC and meet CE/EN15649 recreational craft standards. We perform pre-shipment inspection for seam strength, air leakage, fin box alignment, accessory completeness and packaging. Test reports and certificate scans are available on request.

Do you provide samples before a bulk SUP order? +

Yes. We can send high-resolution photos and short factory videos of the board, paddle and accessories for free. Physical sample boards are available at factory cost plus shipping (usually 1 unit). Sample lead time is 5-10 days.

Who buys these inflatable SUP boards from you? +

Our buyers include watersports retailers, outdoor gear distributors, resort and rental operators, Amazon/Shopify DTC brands, gym/wellness clubs, and TikTok/e-commerce sellers looking for a viral summer product with strong margins.
