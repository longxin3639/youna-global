Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between 120-type and 86-type wall plates? +

120-type (also called Decora or GFCI-size) is the North American standard — approximately 120mm x 70mm rectangular. 86-type is the Chinese/European square standard (86mm x 86mm). These modules are specifically sized for 120-type Decora wall plate frames. If you need 86-type versions, let us know — many of these modules have equivalent 86-type variants available from the same factory.

What is "punch-in" vs "snap-in" vs "solderless"? +

Punch-in (keystone): Tool-free — push wires into spring-loaded terminals. Fastest install.
Snap-in (Lucent style): Spring clips on sides lock the module into the Decora frame. Common for premium HDMI.
Solderless / screw terminal: Wires secured with screws — no soldering iron needed. Used for VGA and some RCA jacks.

How many modules fit in one wall plate? +

Standard single-gang Decora plate fits 1 large module (like the 5-hole+USB) or 2-3 small keystone inserts. Multi-gang plates (2-gang, 3-gang, 4-gang) proportionally fit more. For example, a 3-gang Decora plate can hold up to 6 single-port keystone modules or 3 combo panels. Contact us if you need help designing a custom layout for your project.

Can I mix different brands of 120-type modules? +

Yes — as long as they follow the Decora insert form factor (~49mm x 25mm for single keystone, larger dimensions for combo panels). Our modules use industry-standard dimensions that are compatible with Leviton, Lutron, Legrand, and most major Decora wall plate systems.

Do you sell blank Decora wall plate frames too? +

Yes — we can supply matching blank 120-type frames in white, black, ivory, and brushed metal finishes. We also offer pre-printed or laser-engraved frames with your logo. Add them to your order inquiry and we will include pricing.
