How It Works - Hands-Free Litter Cleaning

1. Cat Uses the Litter Box

Your cat enters and uses the litter box normally. The 4 infrared safety sensors detect the cat's presence and weight. The rake stays still while the cat is inside - no movement, no noise, no stress.

2. Auto-Clean After Cat Leaves

After the cat exits, the system waits a set delay (5-20 minutes, adjustable via app). Then the rake sweeps clumped waste into the sealed waste bin. The entire cleaning cycle takes under 60 seconds.

3. Clean Box, No Odor

The sealed waste bin blocks odors with activated carbon filter + optional fragrance capsule. The litter box stays clean and fresh 24/7. You only need to empty the waste bin every 7-10 days for one cat.

Bonus: App Monitoring

Track your cat's usage frequency and weight (+/-50g) via the free iOS/Android app. Get push notifications when the waste bin is full, when cleaning completes, or if the safety sensors detect an issue. Helps monitor your cat's health.

Frequently Asked Questions

What litter type does it support?

Clumping litter only - bentonite or tofu litter. Non-clumping litter (crystal, pine) will not work with the rake mechanism. We recommend bentonite clay litter (2-5 mm granules) for the most reliable cleaning. We can include a 2 kg starter pack in the box upon request.

Is the app free? Any subscription?

App is 100% free, no subscription. Wi-Fi connection uses your existing home internet. Data plan is NOT required. The app is available in English, German, French, Spanish, and Japanese. OTA firmware updates are included free of charge.

How often do I need to empty the waste bin?

7L sealed bin holds waste for 1 cat for 7-10 days, 2 cats for 4-5 days, 3+ cats for 2-3 days. App sends a "bin full" alert so you never forget. The bin is sealed and odor-blocked - no smell until you open it. Removable bin liner makes disposal easy and hygienic.

Is it safe for kittens?

It is designed for cats 1.5 kg (3.3 lbs) and above. For kittens under 1.5 kg, we recommend keeping them out of the room during the first few months. The 4 infrared safety sensors detect any presence and stop the rake immediately. The entry height is suitable for cats 4+ months old.

What is the warranty and after-sales support?

12-month warranty covers motor, sensors, and main board. We provide spare parts (rake, filter, motor, waste bin) at cost price. Defective units are replaced in your next order - no need to return to China, saving you return shipping costs. Technical support available via WhatsApp and email.

How long does it take for a cat to adapt?

Most cats adapt within 2-7 days. We recommend leaving the unit turned off for the first 2 days so the cat gets used to entering and exiting. Then turn on auto-cleaning with the maximum delay (20 minutes). Gradually reduce the delay as the cat becomes comfortable. Full adaptation guide included in the manual.
