Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ) for loose moissanite stones? +

MOQ is 1 stone for loose moissanite — ideal for small jewelry brands, Etsy/Shopify sellers, engagement-ring makers and dropshippers testing the market. Larger orders (100+ stones) qualify for tiered volume discounts, and you can mix colors, cuts and sizes in one shipment.

Are your moissanite stones certified and how do I verify authenticity? +

Our DEF colorless and fancy-color moissanite come with a GRA certificate and a laser-inscribed girdle code matching the certificate number. Each stone is VVS clarity. We can send the certificate scan and a short video of the laser code before shipment so you can verify authenticity.

What colors of moissanite do you offer? +

We stock DEF colorless plus 24 fancy colors: black, pink, garnet red, orange (Fanta), color-shift blue, light purple, lemon yellow, champagne, light champagne, rainbow, seven-color, AB coating, grey, green, light green, emerald green, blue, light blue, sapphire blue, deep sapphire, light sapphire, sapphire green-base and sea blue. Fancy colors let you build fashion, birthstone and custom jewelry lines most suppliers can't.

What sizes and cuts are available? +

Sizes range from melee (1–5mm) up to 0.5ct, 1ct, 2ct and 3ct+ loose stones. Available cuts include round, oval, cushion, marquise, emerald, pear, heart and princess. Send us your target size/cut list and we will confirm stock and mold availability.

How much does wholesale moissanite from China cost? +

Reference factory prices range from about $3/ct for melee and fancy-color stones up to $60/ct for larger DEF colorless stones, FOB Guangdong. Final pricing depends on size, color, cut, clarity and order quantity. Contact us for a precise quotation tailored to your order.

Do you provide samples before a bulk moissanite order? +

Yes. We can send loose-stone photos and a GRA certificate scan for free, and physical samples (charged at cost + shipping) before bulk production, so you can verify color, cut and brilliance. Sample lead time is typically 3–7 days.

Can you do private label and custom packaging for moissanite? +

Yes. We offer OEM/private-label moissanite with branded packaging (pouches, boxes, insert cards), custom certificates and even set stones into your jewelry. This is our core service for jewelry brands and resellers building a moissanite product line.
