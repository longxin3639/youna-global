Frequently Asked Questions

How does the LED display on the helmet work? +

The rear of the helmet has a built-in LED matrix screen. You connect via Bluetooth using the companion app on your phone (iOS or Android). The app lets you type custom text, choose from preset animations, display turn signals, or upload your own images and GIFs to show on the screen.

Is the helmet safety-certified? +

Yes. The helmet shell meets DOT (US Department of Transportation) and ECE 22.06 standards. The outer shell is made from impact-resistant ABS+PC material with a multi-density EPS foam liner for impact absorption.

How long does the battery last? +

The built-in rechargeable battery lasts approximately 8-10 hours of continuous display use on a single charge. Charging takes about 2-3 hours via USB-C. The display can be turned off independently to conserve battery when not needed.

Can I customize the helmet with my own logo? +

Yes. We offer full OEM customization including custom shell colors, logo printing on the helmet body, branded packaging, and even custom app branding (white-label app). MOQ for custom branding starts at 100 pcs.
