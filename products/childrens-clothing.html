Frequently Asked Questions

Are the fabrics safe for children? +

Yes. All our children's clothing meets Class A safety standards, which is the highest level of textile safety certification in China. This means no harmful dyes, formaldehyde, or heavy metals. We can also arrange third-party testing (SGS, Intertek) upon request.

Can I order mixed styles in one shipment? +

Absolutely. We encourage mixed-style orders to help you test which items sell best. Minimum 200 pcs total, with at least 50 pcs per style/color. This allows you to build a diverse inventory without overcommitting to any single item.

Do you offer sample orders? +

Yes, we can send sample sets (1-3 pcs per style) so you can evaluate fabric quality, sizing, and print before placing a bulk order. Samples are charged at retail price plus shipping, with the cost credited toward your bulk order.

What is the production lead time? +

For in-stock items, orders ship within 5-7 business days. For custom OEM orders with your brand and design, production takes 15-25 business days depending on order volume. Rush orders are available for an additional fee.

Do you ship worldwide? +

Yes. We ship to the USA, Europe, UK, Australia, Canada, Middle East, Southeast Asia, and 20+ other countries. Options include express air (5-10 days), standard air (10-15 days), and sea freight (25-40 days). We also offer DDP shipping with duties prepaid.
