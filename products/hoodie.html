Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between 280 GSM and 380 GSM? +

280 GSM is lightweight fleece, ideal for spring/autumn layering and mild climates. 380 GSM is heavyweight, thick, and plush - designed for cold winters. In between, 320 GSM offers a balanced medium weight for year-round wear in temperate regions.

Can I get a sample before placing a bulk order? +

Yes. We can send sample hoodies in your chosen fabric weight and color. Sample cost is $30-50 per piece plus shipping, which is credited toward your bulk order. Production samples with your custom print are also available.

Do the hoodies come with a zipper? +

The default style is pullover (no zipper), but we also offer full-zip and half-zip (quarter-zip) versions. Zipper hoodies are approximately $1.00-$2.00 more per piece depending on the zipper quality and fabric weight.

What is the production lead time? +

Standard production takes 12-18 business days after artwork approval. Heavyweight fabrics and complex prints may take slightly longer. Rush orders (7-10 days) are available for an additional fee.

Do you ship worldwide? +

Yes. We ship to the USA, Europe, UK, Australia, Canada, Middle East, Southeast Asia, and 20+ other countries. Options include express air (5-10 days), standard air (10-15 days), and sea freight (25-40 days). We also offer DDP shipping with duties prepaid.
