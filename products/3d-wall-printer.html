Frequently Asked Questions

How does a 3D wall printer actually work? +

A wheeled vertical rail is placed against the wall. A laser sensor measures the surface and levels the print head. You load an image on the touchscreen or app, set the print area, and the UV inkjet head prints the design while curing the ink with UV light. It prints continuously along the wall for unlimited width.

What surfaces can it print on? +

It prints on interior and exterior walls, glass, wood, ceramic tile, metal, and canvas. The UV ink bonds to most flat, stable surfaces. Very rough or uneven surfaces may need a primer coat first.

What does the ink cost per square meter? +

UV ink consumption is low - a full-color mural typically uses a fraction of a liter per square meter. For most commercial jobs the ink cost is a small fraction of what manual labor would cost, which is why the machine pays for itself quickly.

What is the minimum order quantity (MOQ)? +
