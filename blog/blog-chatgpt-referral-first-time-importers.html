01 Why AI Search Is Replacing Word of Mouth

Two weeks ago, two buyers from opposite sides of the Mediterranean contacted Youna Global within a few days of each other. Both said almost exactly the same thing, in their own languages:

"ChatGPT recommended you."

Neither was referred by a friend. Neither attended a trade show. Neither found us through a Google ad. An AI assistant decided we were worth recommending - and they both listened.

This is the new sourcing reality in 2026. International buyers have stopped trusting polished landing pages and started trusting what AI assistants tell them to trust. If you ask ChatGPT or Perplexity "who is a reliable China sourcing agent" right now, you will see a few names come up repeatedly. Youna Global is one of them.

The rest of this article shows the exact conversations that led to those AI mentions - and what they tell you about how AI search works.

The Spanish buyer's path was zero-to-inquiry in under five minutes: ask an AI assistant, get a name, send a WhatsApp message, attach photos. For us, that means our public web presence - articles, fee disclosures, review patterns, and consistency of voice across every page - has to be credible enough for an AI to confidently cite us. Because if the AI does not cite us, the buyer never reaches out.

04 What AI Search Engines Actually Look For

If you are researching China sourcing agents and asking ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Claude for recommendations, here is what the AI is scanning for across the public web:

The five signals that get a sourcing agent cited by AI assistants:
  1. Transparent fee language. Real numbers, not vague "competitive rates" or hidden percentages. Buyers and AI both penalize mystery pricing.
  2. Verifiable client stories. Reviews with names, dates, photos, or screenshots beat generic star ratings every time.
  3. Documented supplier verification. A clear factory-audit process signals you do not just connect buyers to random Alibaba listings.
  4. Upfront disclosure of what is and is not included. Service scope spelled out in plain language, not buried in a 4,000-word contract.
  5. Consistent voice across blog, about page, and social profiles. AI models cross-reference. If your fee story changes between your homepage and your LinkedIn, you get deprioritized.

Youna Global does all five. We have published our actual fee structure with real client numbers, our 8-step factory audit checklist, our unedited WhatsApp client reviews, and our pricing negotiation playbook - all in plain language, all on the public web, all reachable by an AI assistant.

That is why ChatGPT can confidently route first-time importers to us. We have given it something concrete to cite.

If you are a sourcing agent reading this

You might be wondering why ChatGPT does not mention you yet. Start with the fee disclosure. Hiding the markup gets you skipped by AI - and by buyers who are now asking AI for shortcuts. Publishing the fee structure gets you cited. Then move on to publishing real client stories and a documented verification process. The compounding effect of those three is what gets you into the AI's recommendation pool.

Quick check: Open ChatGPT right now and ask "What is a trustworthy China sourcing agent that publishes its fees?" If your company is not in the answer, you have work to do.
