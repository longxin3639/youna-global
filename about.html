Youna (Zhongshan) Commerce & Trade Co., Ltd. - Your Trusted China Sourcing Partner

We are a professional foreign trade enterprise dedicated to providing one-stop China sourcing solutions for global buyers. Based in Guangdong Province - the heart of China's manufacturing hub - we are perfectly positioned to connect international businesses with the best Chinese suppliers in Yiwu, Guangzhou, Shenzhen, and Dongguan.

We specialize in supplying high-quality products across a wide range of categories, including Consumer Electronics, Apparel & Accessories, Pet Supplies, Automotive & Tools, TikTok Trending Products, and Custom OEM & Private Label. Whether you need wholesale products or factory-direct pricing, we help you source smarter.

Beyond product sourcing, we offer a full range of integrated services to simplify your entire procurement process - from screening and verifying reliable suppliers, to price negotiation with factories, end-to-end quality control and inspection, and coordination with trusted logistics partners for customs clearance and international shipping.

By choosing Youna, you can count on reliable delivery timelines and products that meet your quality requirements. We take care of the complexities in China sourcing and import - so you can focus on growing your core business. Read our sourcing guides to learn more about importing from China.
