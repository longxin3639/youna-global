## 4. Search Intent Classification

**Primary classification: INFORMATIONAL** (with a commercial-investigation overlay).

**Rationale:**
- **SERP composition:** The top 10 are overwhelmingly educational guides and calculators — no transactional "buy now," no local-service pack, no price-comparison widgets. This signals Google reads the query as research-stage.
- **User mindset:** The searcher has usually already found a supplier quote and is now trying to understand *what the quote does NOT include*. They want a mental model + a checklist, not a vendor yet.
- **Commercial-investigation overlay:** Because the reader is "serious about importing" and our brand is a sourcing agent, a meaningful subset is evaluating *whether* to use an agent and *which* approach. This justifies the "where a transparent agent earns its fee" section and the soft CTA — but the page must NOT read as a sales pitch (that would mismatch the dominant informational intent and hurt rankings).
- **Funnel position:** Top/mid-funnel. This hub feeds the bottom-funnel commercial pages (sourcing-agent cost guide, services) via internal links.

**Intent-by-section mapping (to keep the article honest to intent):**
- Informational core: cost components, worked example, 2026 tariffs, FAQ.
- Commercial-investigation edge: "How to avoid being blindsided" playbook + "where a transparent sourcing agent earns its fee" + soft CTA to services/tariff guide.
