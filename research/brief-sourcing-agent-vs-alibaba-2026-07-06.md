## 4. Search intent classification
Primary: Commercial-investigation (comparison / consideration stage).
Secondary: Informational ("do I even need an agent" / "is it worth it").
What the searcher really wants: a clear, UNBIASED framework to judge which of the three routes fits THEIR situation (experience, order volume, product complexity, budget, risk tolerance). Honest trade-offs, not a pitch. Concrete numbers and an actionable "which should I pick" conclusion. Reassurance that "there's no single right answer" + a path forward.
Tone implication: truly balanced. Cover real downsides of ALL THREE (including using an agent). Bias toward Youna naturally via "most helpful / most transparent," not by bashing Alibaba. CTA as a natural next step, not hard sell.
