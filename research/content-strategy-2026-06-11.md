## 5. Content Writing Guidelines

### What Works (Based on Your PPF Article Performance)
- Specific pricing data ("海外$1,000-$2,000, 直采不到$1,000")
- First-person, on-the-ground tone
- Honest answers ("do you need a sourcing agent? honest answer: depends")
- Concrete checklist formats
- Real photos from your warehouse/factory visits
- Short paragraphs, scannable structure

### What to Avoid
- Content targeting Chinese sellers/exporters
- Generic advice ("choose a reliable supplier") without specifics
- AI clichés ("not all suppliers are created equal", "in today's global economy")
- Absolute promises ("100% guaranteed", "zero risk")

### Title Formula
[Specific Result] + [Timeframe/Number] + [Pain Point Resolution]

Examples:
- "How to Verify a Chinese Factory in 8 Steps (Checklist Included)"
- "Sourcing Agent vs Alibaba: The Real Cost Comparison Nobody Shows You"
- "7 Hidden Costs of Importing from China That Eat Your Margin"
