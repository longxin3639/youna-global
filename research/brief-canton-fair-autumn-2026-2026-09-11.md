## 2. Competitor landscape

**Top 3 ranking-style competitor pages:**

1. yiwubuying.com - "Canton Fair Autumn 2026 The Complete Buyer's Guide" - approx 1,800 to 2,200 words. Direct keyword overlap with ours. Covers 10 first-time tips, a before-you-go form, badge steps, hotels, VPN, and money. Weakness: generic listicle, no phase strategy, no post-fair plan.
2. cantonfair.co - "Canton Fair First-Time Visitor Tips" - approx 1,400 to 1,800 words. Covers registration, what to bring, navigation, WeChat and Alipay, business-card etiquette. Weakness: timeless tips, not tied to the current session, no sourcing depth.
3. sourcingyuan.com - "Canton Fair Guide: How to Prepare for Your First Visit as an International Buyer" - approx 1,600 to 2,000 words. Covers visa, business materials, packing, digital payments, and a 12-week timeline. Weakness: no category-to-phase mapping, no supplier verification, no agent angle.

**Common themes all top pages cover (must-cover for us too):**
- Online pre-registration and the buyer badge
- Visa and invitation letter basics
- Where to stay near Pazhou
- VPN, WeChat, Alipay setup
- What to bring and wear
- Business cards and booth etiquette
- A basic phase overview

**Content gaps we can own (our differentiation):**
1. Session-specific detail. Competitors reuse older templates or a generic "autumn" page. We name the 140th session, the exact 2026 dates, the themes, and the new special zones.
2. A phase-to-category decision table so the reader picks the correct week for their product instead of guessing.
3. A pre-fair countdown that folds in supplier shortlisting and booth research, not just travel logistics.
4. The fair-to-order handoff: the follow-up window, samples, MOQ negotiation, and QC. Generic blogs end at the badge and never explain how a booth contact becomes an order.
5. The sourcing-agent angle: attending on the reader's behalf and verifying booths on the floor. This is the commercial differentiator no competitor covers.

**Differentiation positioning:** "The only Autumn 2026 prep guide written by someone who will actually be on the floor." Forward-looking, category-led, and connected to what happens after the fair.
