**Verified market data usable in the draft (attribute each to its source):**
- 1688 generally shows tiered pricing around these bands: 2 to 99 units, 100 to 499 units, and 500+ units. Spot stock can start as low as 2 or 3 units.
- Adding your own logo usually pushes the minimum to 500 units or more; customization and low MOQ are usually mutually exclusive in most categories.
- Platform MOQ comparison (typical ranges): 1688 about 10 to 200 units (spot stock as low as 2); Alibaba 100 to 1,000+; Made-in-China 200 to 1,000; Global Sources 300 to 5,000.
- 1688 does not accept international credit cards or PayPal; it runs on Alipay or WeChat Pay, which normally require a Chinese bank account, and suppliers ship to mainland China addresses only. Negotiation happens on Aliwangwang.
- Chinese sources report that a large majority of 1688 suppliers support one-piece dropshipping and small-batch mixing, which is why 1688 is the default test channel for small sellers.
- TikTok trending-product research tools: TikTok Creative Center (official), plus third-party tools Kalodata, FastMoss, PipiAds, Shoplus, and BigSpy. Douyin trends often lead overseas markets by roughly 6 to 12 months.
- Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) inbound windows are short, so source speed is a hard constraint, not a preference.
- Representative small-batch cost example (attribute as a market example, not a quote): a 500-unit consumer order shipped from Guangzhou to Los Angeles lands near a few thousand US dollars, with product cost well under half of the total landed cost; inspection, freight, duty, brokerage, and last-mile make up the rest.

- Recommended meta description (target 150 to 160 characters):

### 5.4 Body angle

- Current angle (on disk, already rewritten): a six-step sourcing system for overseas sellers, with the 1688 access reality, a three-stage test model, FBT back-timing, a cost example, and FAQ.
- Recommended target angle (confirm or refine to this):

- Body items to fix if refining the current draft:
  - Remove or rephrase any percentage pricing claim that could read as our own markup or fee. Keep only platform-level or supplier-level market data clearly attributed to a third-party source, and keep our own pricing language to "transparent pricing" or "Inquire for Price."
