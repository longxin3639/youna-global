## 1. Humanization Analysis (15/20)

### What's Working (Green Flags)

1. **Brisbane opener (Section 1):** "a distributor in Brisbane told me he'd been paying $1,800 per roll... his jaw literally dropped" — vivid, specific, personal. This is the strongest human moment in the article.

2. **James from Melbourne (Section 3):** A named persona with a concrete scenario ($1,050 → $320 per roll, $100K/year saved). Readers can project themselves into this story.

3. **Toronto distributor (Section 5):** Named city, specific timeline ("early 2025"), specific margin shift (35% → 70%), concrete outcome ("outsold the name brand in his own shop within six months").

4. **"I've seen importers go straight from sample approval to a 500-roll order"** (Section 4) — First-person experience, not generic advice.

5. **"Automotive film isn't a 'ship it and forget it' product"** (Section 6) — Conversational metaphor that sounds like someone who's dealt with customs headaches.

6. **Brand names dropped naturally:** XPEL, 3M, SunTek, Lubrizol, BASF, Huntsman — these aren't decorative; they're reference points professionals use in real conversations.
