## 二、AI 痕迹检测与修改建议（7 处原文 → 修改后）

> 判定口径：套话/公式化对比、空洞抽象短语、生硬关键词插入、超长单句。以下给出可落地的英文对照，Phase 5 直接套用。

**1. L79 — "a spectrum, not a binary" 套路**
- ORIGINAL: *"But 'factory' in China is a spectrum, not a binary. I've visited legitimate 10,000-square-meter plants with 400 workers, and 'factories' that are really a warehouse with a sticker gun."*
- REVISED: *"But 'factory' in China isn't yes-or-no. I've walked into legitimate 10,000-square-meter plants with 400 workers — and into 'factories' that were really a warehouse with a sticker gun."*

**2. L22 — 公式化 "isn't about X, it's about Y" + 关键词串**
- ORIGINAL: *"The manufacturer or trading company china decision isn't about a 'good' versus 'bad' option — both have a place. It's about matching the right structure to your order, risk tolerance, and stage of growth."*
- REVISED: *"Neither side is 'right' by default — both earn their place. The smart move is matching the structure to your order size, how much risk you'll tolerate, and where your business stands today."*

**3. L112 — "The lesson isn't X. It's: Y" 模板**
- ORIGINAL: *"The lesson isn't 'never use a trader.' It's: know who you're paying, price the hidden risk, and verify before you commit."*
- REVISED: *"So the takeaway isn't 'never use a trader.' It's simpler: know who's actually in the deal, put a number on the risk you can't see, and verify before you wire a deposit."*

**4. L185 — "stops being X and starts being Y" 对仗**
- ORIGINAL: *"When volume and customization rise, the factory stops being 'risky' and starts being 'obvious.'"*
- REVISED: *"Push volume and customization high enough, and going direct stops feeling like a gamble — at some point it's just the only sensible call."*

**5. L103 — 抽象短语 "the math flips"**
- ORIGINAL: *"This is where the factory-vs-trader math flips:"*
- REVISED: *"This is where buying from a trader quietly starts costing more than it saves:"*

**6. L49 — "love to frame X as villains" 套话 + 三连形容词**
- ORIGINAL: *"Forums love to frame trading companies as villains, but a trading company is a normal, legal, and often useful part of Chinese supply chains."*
- REVISED: *"Forums like to paint traders as the bad guys. They aren't. A trading company is a normal, legal link in the chain — and sometimes the smartest one to use."*

**7. L163 — 生硬长尾关键词插入**
- ORIGINAL: *"This is the heart of the china sourcing manufacturer or trading company for small business question: it's not permanent. It's a stage."*
- REVISED: *"That's the real lesson for small businesses: this isn't a permanent decision. It's just a stage you move through as you grow."*

> 附：em-dash（—）全文约 6 处，多落在表格单元格与同位语（L18/L22/L33/L37/L41/L110），属合理使用，**不构成重度 AI 痕迹**；仅 L110 的 "— roughly $38,000 in lost margin" 若拆分句子可顺势改为句号，其余保留。

## 六、品牌声音校准（Karsa 人设核查）

**一致处（✓ 达标）**：
- 第一人称稳定："my decade walking Guangdong factory floors"（L20）、"I'll push back"（L167）、"I drove there to verify"（L136）、"my team at Youna Global"（L189）——Karsa 实地代理人格贯穿。
- **不贬低贸易公司**：L49 明确"不是坏人"、L165–174 专设"贸易公司更聪明的 4 种情形"、L112 结论"不是永远别用 trader"——平衡基调到位。
- 证据导向：每个论点配数字/故事（3 个具名迷你故事 + 法规日期），符合"重证据"设定。
- 收尾 CTA 以"免费验厂""算账""选路线"三步走，helper 而非硬销。

**偏离 / 需修正处**：
- **a) Agent 节略偏推销（L187–199）**："A trading company profits from the gap… opacity helps them" 语气带对抗，与"不贬低贸易商"基调微冲突。建议改为中性："A trader's margin comes from that gap; an agent's fee is separate and visible" —— 讲机制而非贴标签。
- **b) L193 "NewBuyingAgent makes the same point"**：外部引用薄弱且可能虚构，破坏 Karsa"讲事实"的可信人设。建议核实或替换为真实可查来源（如公开行业报告），否则删去机构名，改"行业共识也指出…"。
- **c) L55 "ChineseCheck, a sourcing-research firm, cites this range"**：同上，需事实核查，避免上线后成漏洞。
- **d) L211 时序不一致**：文内设定为 2026-07，却写 "detentions are expected to keep rising through 2025–2026"——2025 已成过去。改为 "continue rising through 2026 and into 2027" 或 "keep climbing in 2026"，与全文 2026 语境一致。
- **e) L236 轻微 AI 腔对仗**："verify first, decide deliberately, and re-decide as they scale" 与 "this order, this quarter, this stage" 属可接受的人性化修辞，但全文已有多处同类三连，建议将后者改为更口语的 "for this order, this quarter, wherever you are right now" 以削弱模板感。
- **f) CTA 推广浓度（L197 / L242–244）**：转化区出现 3 条 Youna 内链，属合理；仅建议锚文本保持多样（已做到：checklist / cost guide / vs-Alibaba），勿再加第四条，避免结尾变广告。

**品牌声音总评**：人设高度一致，仅需在 a/b/c/d 四处做"去推销化 + 引述可信化 + 时序修正"，即可完全贴合 Karsa 务实、平衡、第一人称可信的调性。
