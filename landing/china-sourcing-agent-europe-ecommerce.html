Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from European sellers making the switch to China inventory sourcing.

I have never done a bulk order from China before. Is this the right approach for me?
Yes — and Karsa specialises in first-time bulk buyers. You do not need to understand the Chinese manufacturing ecosystem or speak Mandarin. You describe your product, Karsa handles everything from sourcing to shipping, and you approve at each stage before committing funds.
What is the minimum order size?
It depends on the product — most factories have MOQs between 50 and 500 units for a first order. Karsa specifically looks for suppliers willing to work with smaller initial batches (50-200 units) so you can test before scaling. For high-value products, even smaller quantities are often negotiable.
How long does sea freight from China to Europe take?
Sea freight (LCL) from Guangdong to Northwest Europe (Rotterdam, Hamburg, Antwerp) typically takes 25-35 days. For Southern Europe (Italy, Spain) add 3-5 days. Air freight takes 5-7 days if you need speed. Karsa provides accurate estimates for your specific destination.
Do I need an EORI number to import from China?
Yes — if you are importing commercially as a business in the EU, you need an EORI (Economic Operators Registration and Identification) number. It is free and easy to register with your national customs authority. Karsa can guide you on what documents to prepare, but recommends working with a local customs broker or freight forwarder in your country for the EU import declaration itself.
What are Karsa's fees?
Karsa charges 3% commission on the order value, payable only when you confirm the purchase. There is no upfront fee for sourcing, supplier research, or sample evaluation. If you do not proceed with an order, there is no charge. Shipping and freight costs are separate (passed through at cost).
Do you ship to all EU countries?
Yes — Karsa ships to all EU member states, as well as the UK, Norway, Switzerland, and other European destinations. Whether you need delivery to a personal address, a 3PL warehouse, or an Amazon FBA centre in Europe, Karsa can arrange the appropriate freight and documentation.
