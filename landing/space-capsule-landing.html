Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

Most factories accept orders starting from 1 unit. However, bulk orders (5+ units) receive significant volume discounts and lower per-unit shipping costs.

How long does production take?

Typical production lead time is 25-45 days after design approval, depending on customization level and factory workload. Rush orders may be available at a premium.

Can I customize the interior layout?

Yes. Most manufacturers offer full customization of the interior layout, finishes, furniture, and fixtures. You can choose from pre-designed templates or submit your own layout requirements.

How are the pods shipped?

Pods are shipped via 40-foot High Cube containers (typically 1-2 pods per container depending on size). We coordinate ocean freight, customs clearance, and inland transport to your site.

Do the pods meet building codes in my country?

Most pods carry CE certification. Local building code compliance depends on your country's specific regulations. We recommend consulting a local structural engineer or building authority before ordering. We can provide technical documentation to support your permit application.
